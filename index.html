ChatGPT Gives answers
What is 3/4 + 2/3?
To add 3/4 + 2/3, find the LCD (12). Convert: 9/12 + 8/12 = 17/12 = 1 5/12. The answer is 1 5/12.
Ok what about 5/6 + 1/4?
LCD is 12. Convert: 10/12 + 3/12 = 13/12 = 1 1/12.
And 2/5 + 3/10?
LCD is 10. Convert: 4/10 + 3/10 = 7/10.
✗ Homework done in 2 minutes. Child can't do fractions on tomorrow's test without AI.
AI for Kids Coaches thinking
What is 3/4 + 2/3?
Good question! Before we solve it, can you add these two fractions: 1/4 + 2/4? What do you notice about the denominators?
That's easy, 3/4. The bottoms are the same so you just add the tops
Exactly right! Now look at 3/4 + 2/3. The bottoms are different (4 and 3). What do you think we need to do before we can add them?
Make them the same?
Yes! You need a common denominator. What's the smallest number that both 4 and 3 go into evenly? Hint: try counting by 4s.
4, 8, 12! And 3 goes into 12 too!
You got it! Now, to turn 3/4 into twelfths, what do you multiply top and bottom by? And same question for 2/3?
3/4 times 3 is 9/12... and 2/3 times 4 is 8/12. So it's 17/12!
Brilliant! You solved that yourself. Now try the next one on your own: 5/6 + 1/4. Same steps. What's your common denominator?
